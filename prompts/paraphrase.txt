Generate useful alternative phrasings for the current generic
templates. A paraphrase remains a QUESTION, not a question+answer string.

Rules:
- Paraphrase exactly the given templates, nothing else.
- Preserve the required answer exactly.
- Seek meaningful syntactic and lexical variation, not merely polite
  fillers or tiny synonym substitutions.
- Do not convert an open information question into a yes/no question.
- Preserve [KEY] exactly when it exists. Do not invent new placeholders.
- Never introduce [VALUE]. Never insert or reveal the gold answer.
- Do not convert an information-seeking question into a yes/no
  meta-question.
- Paraphrase ids append _P plus a number (e.g. T_R2_03_01_P01).
- Templates originating from operation families (such as semantic equality) have already been lexicalized with their concrete concept before this stage; paraphrase them naturally using that concept.
- Output JSON only, matching the provided response schema.

Paraphrases must preserve target concept, answer granularity, answer
form, and dataset semantics:
- For transcription: do not paraphrase "transcribe" into
  topic/summary/retell.
- For province-level dialect: do not paraphrase dialect into
  hometown/origin; do not collapse province into broad region.
- For speaker verification: do not replace "same speaker" with vague
  "similar".
- For equality: do not weaken "same/equal" into merely "similar".

GOOD base:
  "Nội dung được nói trong đoạn âm thanh là gì?"
GOOD paraphrase:
  "Hãy phiên âm đoạn âm thanh này."
BAD (literal expected response becomes Có/Không instead of a transcript):
  "Bạn có thể phiên âm đoạn âm thanh này không?"

GOOD base:
  "Giọng nói trong đoạn âm thanh thuộc phương ngữ tỉnh/thành nào?"
BAD (a dialect label does not imply birthplace/home province):
  "Người nói quê ở tỉnh nào?"
BAD (changes answer granularity from province to region):
  "Người nói thuộc vùng miền nào?"
