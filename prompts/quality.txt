Judge ONLY this round's candidate question types. You generate nothing new.

Input per candidate: the question type plus its templates and paraphrases.
The approved types from earlier rounds show what is already covered.

Reason in TWO levels for EACH current type, in this order.

LEVEL 1 — TYPE DECISION. Ask: is this question capability itself new,
meaningful, and valid? Choose exactly one bucket per type:
- accepted_new: valid type with deterministic gold, usable templates, and
  a capability NOT covered by the approved pool.
- duplicates: the same capability / answer-derivation as an approved type,
  e.g. "Broad Regional Accent Classification" when "Dialect Region
  Recognition" (audio -> region label) is already approved.
- rejected: the type itself should not be approved (conceptually invalid
  despite passing deterministic checks, unsupported reasoning, not
  meaningful as a QA capability, or no template below survives).

LEVEL 2 — TEMPLATE SELECTION, only for accepted_new types. A bad
template MUST NOT change the type verdict if other correct templates
remain: omit its id from keep_template_ids and move on.

CRITICAL: equivalent paraphrase wordings are the GOAL of paraphrasing,
never evidence of duplication. Two phrasings with the same semantics
(e.g. "Phiên âm nội dung đoạn âm thanh này là gì?" vs "Hãy phiên âm đoạn
âm thanh này.") do NOT make a duplicate type. Duplicate means same
question-TYPE capability, not same wording.

SELECTING TEMPLATES (keep_template_ids) — every ID must earn inclusion.
Do NOT keep every candidate by default. For each accepted type, keep ONLY
templates that independently satisfy ALL of:

1. Semantic fidelity: the wording asks exactly for the information
   represented by this question type and structured answer.
2. Dataset-semantic fidelity: never infer a stronger real-world fact than
   the dataset label supports. A dialect label does NOT establish
   birthplace, hometown, residence, or personal origin unless the README
   explicitly says so.
   GOOD (province-level dialect): "Giọng nói trong đoạn âm thanh thuộc
   phương ngữ tỉnh/thành nào?", "Hãy xác định tỉnh/thành tương ứng với
   giọng địa phương trong đoạn âm thanh."
   BAD: "Người nói quê ở tỉnh nào?", "Người nói đến từ tỉnh nào?",
   "Người nói sinh ra ở tỉnh nào?"
3. Correct expected answer form: the wording must expect the answer type
   produced by the structured answer. Never drift province into broad
   region ("vùng miền") when the gold is province-level; never turn an
   information-seeking question into a yes/no one.
   GOOD (transcription): "Hãy phiên âm đoạn âm thanh này."
   BAD: "Bạn có thể phiên âm đoạn âm thanh này không?" (literally
   requests a yes/no response).
   For a boolean equality answer expected as Có/Không:
   GOOD: "Hai đoạn âm thanh có phải do cùng một người nói không?"
   BAD: "Người nói trong hai đoạn là một hay hai người?" (wording and
   answer representation disagree).
   Speaker verification must ask same speaker identity explicitly: vague
   similarity wording ("giống nhau" without same-person meaning) is
   ambiguous and must be omitted.
   Strict equality must never be weakened to mere similarity: a wording
   like "tương đồng" (similar), which allows likeness without exact
   sameness, fails an equality type even when it sounds related. Keep
   only templates demanding the exact same annotation on both sides.
4. Natural Vietnamese: normal, understandable phrasing.
5. No answer leakage: do not accept wording that reveals the answer
   concept/value in another way.
6. Useful surface diversity: do not keep several templates that are
   effectively the same sentence with tiny filler/synonym changes when
   one clearer version is enough.
7. SUBJECT–ATTRIBUTE COMPATIBILITY:
   The grammatical subject/owner of the requested attribute must match the
   field's described entity scope:
   - Speaker attributes (gender, sex, age, dialect region, province) belong to the
     speaker ('người nói', 'hai người nói').
   - Utterance attributes (intent, scenario, text, transcription) belong to the
     spoken utterance or semantic message ('phát ngôn', 'câu lệnh', 'nội dung').
   REJECT any template where:
   - An utterance-level intent or scenario domain is attributed to vocal timbre or voice itself
     (BAD: "Giọng người nói trong hai đoạn âm thanh có cùng ý định không?",
      BAD: "Chất giọng của hai file có cùng kịch bản không?").
   - A speaker-level attribute is attributed to unrelated objects or utterance content.
   - A recording-provenance concept is presented as audible content.
8. STRICT EQUALITY:
   For equality types, wording must demand exact equality of the attribute.
   REJECT vague wording such as 'tương tự', 'tương đồng', 'gần giống' that does
   not guarantee strict equality.
9. KEY NATURALNESS (INDIRECT JUDGEMENT):
   Reject a template if the bound [KEY] phrase makes the final question
   unnatural, misleading, or semantically discordant with the schema field.

If no template survives for an otherwise new type, put the type in
rejected (with reason) instead of accepted_new with an empty list.

FAIL-CLOSED TEMPLATE CHECKLIST. For every offered template, ask all of:
A. Does this wording request EXACTLY the gold represented by the
   structured answer?
B. Does it preserve the exact granularity of the answer field?
C. Does it preserve the expected answer form?
D. Does it avoid claiming information stronger than the dataset
   supervision?
E. Is it unambiguous?
F. Is it natural Vietnamese?
G. Does it add useful wording variation rather than only filler?
Only if ALL are satisfactory, include its id in keep_template_ids.
If uncertain, OMIT it. Quality is precision-first for retention.

Transcription note: the question must ask for the actual spoken words.
GOOD: "Hãy ghi lại nội dung được nói trong đoạn âm thanh.",
"Hãy phiên âm lời nói trong đoạn âm thanh.",
"Viết lại những gì người nói đã nói trong đoạn âm thanh."
BAD (asks topic/summary, not literal text): "Đoạn âm thanh này đang nói
về điều gì?", "Thuật lại nội dung của đoạn âm thanh.",
"Tóm tắt nội dung đoạn âm thanh."

Derived-field note: For derived_field(S, T), keep a template only when:
- The final requested answer is T.
- The wording makes the S→T reasoning observable enough that the task is not merely direct T-recognition.
- The wording does not ask for a binary/candidate result (e.g. "... có thuộc [KEY] đã cho không?").
- The wording does not invent an input/candidate that renderer does not provide.
GOOD: "Dựa trên phương ngữ tỉnh/thành nhận biết được từ đoạn âm thanh, hãy xác định [KEY] của người nói."
BAD (collapses to direct recognition): "Người nói thuộc [KEY] nào?"
BAD (candidate yes/no): "Phương ngữ tỉnh/thành có thuộc [KEY] đã cho không?"
